# 0. Overview

Community-contributed pathway: **Savyasachi (Savya)**

**Sector:** Productivity / Founder Tools

**Problem:** Early-stage Indian founders and operators; secondary: freelancers and consultants; expansion: small agency owners

**Approach:** Savyasachi is a pre-pilot AI personal assistant for Indian founders and operators. It converts voice memos and calls into structured actions across Notion, Google Calendar, Gmail, and WhatsApp using MCP agents. No live users yet — currently recruiting 10 founding users.

# 3. Micro-Innovations

## Persona

**1. 1. The current workaround is unnamed (Persona × Explore — relates to Unit 1)**
The pitch names the pain (context lost across tools, founder as connective tissue) but doesn't document what founders actually do today instead — which specific tools, what sequence, what partial fix they've already tried. The workaround is the real baseline. Without it, the replacement comparison is assumed, not evidenced.

## Solution

**2. 4. No alternative-to-AI comparison documented (Solution × Explore — relates to Unit 2)**
The pitch compares Savya to a human EA on cost, but does not document whether a simpler non-AI solution (a structured Notion template, a Zapier workflow, a dedicated VA) was genuinely considered and rejected — and why. This is the AI-fit justification the next adopter needs.

## Ecosystem

**4. 6. WhatsApp-native distribution as a market-entry condition, not a feature choice**
- **Dimension:** Ecosystem
- **Stage:** Explore
- **Type:** Strategic Decision
- **Decision:** Treat WhatsApp integration as a non-negotiable distribution condition for the Indian founder market, not an optional UX enhancement. The product is designed around the assumption that founders will only engage with AI-generated outputs if they arrive inside WhatsApp.
- **Alternative considered:** Not documented in the source.
- **Why:** With 9.4 apps juggled daily by the target persona, adding another dashboard or review interface would almost certainly be ignored. WhatsApp is where Indian founders already make decisions. Meeting them there is the distribution strategy.
- **What this looked like here:** WhatsApp notification layer is part of V1 — not a V2 addition. The pitch explicitly names it as a market differentiator, alongside Indian-market pricing and founder-built context.
- **Condition — applies when:** WhatsApp is the dominant async communication layer for the target user base. Fails when entering a market or user segment where WhatsApp is not primary (e.g. enterprise users in regulated sectors).
- **Also relevant at:** Define — the WhatsApp API integration (Business API vs. unofficial methods) is a non-trivial architectural dependency that must be resolved before pilot.
