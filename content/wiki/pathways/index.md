# Pathways

Seven pathway concepts, each synthesized from executive summaries,
extended pathway documents, and interviews, and structured around the
four dimensions × four stages framework (`content/framework.md`).

# Voice AI

* [MahaVISTAAR](mahavistaar.md) - Maharashtra's state-run voice advisory line for farmers, at Scale.
* [Bhili Language Enablement for Voice AI](bhili-language-enablement.md) - bringing a zero-resource tribal language into voice AI, at Pilot.
* [Voice AI for Inclusion](voice-ai-for-inclusion.md) - cross-deployment synthesis across MahaVISTAAR, BharatVISTAAR, Amul/Sarlaben, and others, at Scale.
* [Voice AI Adoption Barriers](voice-ai-adoption-barriers.md) - why organizations don't adopt Voice AI even when subsidised, an Explore-stage diagnostic.

# Livelihoods and Data

* [Blue Dots](blue-dots.md) - open voice-AI discovery infrastructure for local jobs, talent, and services, at Scale.
* [Data DHARA](data-dhara.md) - turning fragmented government records into machine-readable systems, at Define.

# Climate and Public Health

* [CEEW Climate Intelligence](ceew-climate-intelligence.md) - conversational climate-data access and a dengue early-warning system, at Pilot.
